# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces must remain professional, respectful, and safe. Constructive
technical disagreement is welcome. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct,
doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publication of private
repository material or credentials are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, security reports,
and other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an established
private channel. Do not publish sensitive evidence in an issue. The maintainer may
edit or remove content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation,
or permanently ban a participant when needed to protect the project and its
community.

Reports will be reviewed for context and handled as privately as practical.
Retaliation against a person who raises a good-faith concern is prohibited.
